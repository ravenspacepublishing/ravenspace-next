We recognize that RavenSpace is located on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the Musqueam | xʷməθkʷəy̓əm people. This land was and continues to be of great importance to the First Nations peoples of this region.

Many of our activities take place digitally and we are connected by communication technologies and the winds that blow from each direction. Joining up from different places, we acknowledge and respect Indigenous peoples’ connection to their territories.
