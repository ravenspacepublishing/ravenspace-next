---
title: The Cherokee Natural World
description: >-
  As our bonds to the planet’s web of life are strained and broken by
  exploitation and neglect, The Cherokee Natural World provides a different
  vision of our place in the world, one rooted in harmonious relationships that
  are crucial to our future. This remarkable, visually stunning digital
  publication offers the largest collection of terms, stories, and reflections
  ever gathered together in one place from knowledge keepers of the Cherokee
  People, the second-largest Indigenous nation in the United States.
